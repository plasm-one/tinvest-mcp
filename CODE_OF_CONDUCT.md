# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where people can ask a naive question about
a brokerage API without being made to feel stupid, and where a disagreement
about a risk check is settled by reasoning rather than by seniority.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith.
* Be specific. "This check can be bypassed when X" helps; "this is broken"
  does not.
* Accept that maintainers will decline things, and that a decline is not a
  judgement of you. The reasons are written down in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
* Take responsibility for mistakes, fix them, and move on without ceremony.
* Remember that this project touches people's money. A confident wrong answer
  in an issue thread can cost a reader real money — say what you are unsure
  about.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
* Sexualised language or imagery; unwelcome sexual attention.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Posting someone else's API token, account number, or portfolio.
* Deliberately misleading advice about the security of this software, or
  encouraging others to disable safety controls they do not understand.
* Using issues or discussions to promote investment schemes, signal services,
  or anything soliciting money from readers.

## Financial advice

Nobody here — maintainers included — is your financial advisor. Do not ask for
personal investment recommendations in issues or discussions, and do not give
them. Questions about how the software behaves are always welcome; questions
about what you should buy are not what this project is for. See
[DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md).

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to <alex@plasm.one>. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers will respond in a way proportionate to the
circumstances: a private word, a public correction, a temporary block, or a
permanent ban for repeated or severe violations.

Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face consequences
determined by other members of the project's leadership.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, code review — and when representing the project in public.
